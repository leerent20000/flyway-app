Flyway privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Flyway has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Everything you create stays on your iPhone. To show weather and maps, Flyway sends the location of the area you are looking at to Canadian government data services and to Apple, and nothing else.

What Flyway stores on your iPhone

Waypoints and folders, recorded GPS tracks, drawings, journal entries, bird observations, photos you attach, downloaded offline map areas, and your settings.

This information is stored only on your device. Flyway has no server and does not upload it anywhere. It may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), according to your device settings.

Deleting Flyway deletes everything it stores.

Location

With your permission, Flyway uses your location to show where you are on the map, to point the map the way you are facing, and to give weather and conditions for where you are.

If you allow “Always”, Flyway uses your location in the background only while you are recording a track, so the recording continues with the screen off. It stops when you stop the recording. Flyway does not track you at any other time.

You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in the Settings app.

Camera and photos

Flyway uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to a waypoint or journal entry. Attached photos are copied into Flyway’s own storage on your device and are not uploaded.

What is sent off your iPhone

To fetch weather, radar, observations and water levels, Flyway sends a coordinate, or the edges of the map area on screen, to Environment and Climate Change Canada (weather.gc.ca).

To draw map layers such as Wildlife Management Units, Crown land, wetlands and topographic maps, Flyway sends the edges of the map area on screen to Land Information Ontario and to Government of Canada mapping services (including Natural Resources Canada and Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada).

The base map and place search are provided by Apple Maps, under Apple’s privacy policy.

These requests contain no name, account, device identifier or other information about you. Like any internet request, they reveal your device’s IP address to the service that answers them. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

What Flyway does not do

Flyway does not collect personal information, require an account, use analytics or advertising, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share your data with anyone.

Exports and backups you make

When you export a backup, GPX or KML file, Flyway writes it where you choose. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Children

Flyway is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and on this page, with a new effective date.
